# Plan ledger + slot provenance (airline E4, 2026-11-02)

Mode `ledger` (`tau2_rules.py`): (1) when the customer says yes to an agent proposal, deepseek-chat lists the database
actions the proposal commits to; when the agent later writes a closing/text message while some of them have not been
executed (and some write already happened), the reasoner regenerates the turn with a "plan ledger" note; (2) a
`book_reservation` with insurance or checked bags the customer never asked for (or declined) is regenerated by the
reasoner with a "booking attribute" note. On top of E4.

| cut | E4 + ledger | E4 control |
|---|---|---|
| 33 failures, first divergence | 17 / 33 | 16 / 33 (PONR run) |
| 32 successes, right before the decisive write (harm check) | 31 / 32 | 30 / 32 (write-gate control) |

Paired on failures: 6 better / 5 worse. No effect, no harm. The ledger fired 5 times in 4 episodes (61 steps recorded),
the slot check never fired.

Why: of the 16 failed continuations, 10 again miss a gold write, and in **9 of those 10 the agent told the customer the
action was not allowed** (tasks 23, 29: cancellation refused although the gold cancels; 44: flight change refused). The
step was never agreed, so there is nothing for a ledger to enforce. The "missing write" class in CALC_ERRORS.md is
therefore mostly **wrong refusals** (eligibility misjudged), not forgotten steps: the bookkeeping share stated there
(57%) is overstated; corrected reading: eligibility judgments ≈ the largest class, unexecuted agreed steps are rare.

Next lever implied: a deterministic eligibility executor (cancellation: booked < 24 h, business, airline-cancelled,
insurance + covered reason; change: basic economy, flown segments) computed from the reservation facts and shown to
the agent before it answers an eligibility question, so "allowed" is not left to the LLM. Zero-cost audit first: how
often a refusal contradicts the computed eligibility in failures vs successes.
